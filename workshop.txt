title=ProjectHoomans WIP
description=Project Hoomans
description=Bring the large-scale NPC, faction, and settlement gameplay of Project Zomboid Build 45 into Build 42.
description=Project Hoomans is a large-scale NPC and colony simulation system focused on making the world feel alive. NPCs aren't simply spawned around the player to perform a few scripted tasks. 
description=
description=they can live, work, build, socialize, fight, trade, form relationships, and participate in the world alongside you.
description=The goal is to create a dynamic survival experience where your community develops its own stories and behaviors instead of feeling like a collection of static game mechanics.
description=
description=⚔️ Factions & Conflict
description=    • Faction Wars between both NPC and player factions.
description=    • NPC factions can fight, cooperate, trade, recruit, and interact with one another.
description=    • Designed to support faction-based mods such as Bandits and Necroa.
description=    • Dynamic visitors, looters, traders, and other groups can interact with settlements.
description=
description=👥 Living NPCs
description=NPCs are persistent inhabitants of the world. They don't simply exist inside menus or abstract systems. They can physically live and operate on the map.
description=They can:
description=    • Move around the world.
description=    • Work at assigned facilities.
description=    • Build and maintain structures.
description=    • Gather resources.
description=    • Cook and prepare food.
description=    • Farm and harvest crops.
description=    • Fish.
description=    • Craft and smelt.
description=    • Research technologies.
description=    • Haul corpses and materials.
description=    • Scavenge for supplies.
description=    • Socialize with other NPCs.
description=    • Visit other settlements.
description=    • Trade with players and NPCs.
description=    • Develop relationships with people around them.
description=NPC behavior is driven by their needs, skills, traits, relationships, tasks, priorities, and current situation.
description=
description=❤️ Deep Relationship System 
description=An in-depth relationship system inspired by relationship-focused survival games such as the Survivalist series.
description=NPCs can develop different opinions and relationships toward:
description=    • The player.
description=    • Other NPCs.
description=    • Factions.
description=    • Groups and communities.
description=Relationships can develop over time based on interactions and events rather than being a simple friendship meter.
description=
description=🧠 NPC Traits & Skills
description=Every NPC can have different characteristics that influence how they behave.
description=Traits
description=Traits can affect both physical and social behavior, including how an NPC communicates, reacts to situations, performs tasks, and interacts with others.
description=Skills
description=NPC skills determine what work they can effectively perform. An NPC with the right skills can be assigned to the appropriate facility and work independently.
description=
description=💬 NPC Conversation
description=Project Hoomans includes a lightweight **natural-language conversation system inspired by the approach used by **Façade.
description=Rather than requiring an LLM for every conversation, the system can use in-game variables, context, relationships, traits, and authored language rules to produce coherent NPC dialogue.
description=For players who want to take conversations further, supporters can also use my custom helper application to enhance NPC conversations with:
description=    • LLM-powered dialogue
description=    • Audio TTS
description=    • Speaker-aware voices
description=    • More dynamic conversations
description=The LLM layer is designed to be optional. You can play with the game's built-in conversation system without requiring an external LLM.
description=
description=🏗️ Base Building & NPC Construction
description=NPCs can participate in building and maintaining settlements.
description=They can construct both:
description=    • Base-game structures
description=    • Modded construction content
description=Depending on their available skills, facilities, materials, and assigned work.
description=
description=⛺ Camp System
description=Don't have a permanent base yet?
description=The camp system provides a way for players to establish temporary living arrangements before committing to a full settlement.
description=

description=🔎 Scavenging & Foraging
description=A dedicated scavenging and foraging system is being developed, including the possibility of random encounters inspired by games such as Sheltered.
description=The current scavenging implementation is primarily being used for testing and as a placeholder while the larger system is developed.
description=
description=🔬 Research
description=A research system is being developed to give settlements their own technological progression.
description=Currently, the research lifecycle is implemented, with the broader technology progression still under development.
description=
description=📦 Upgradable Storage
description=Settlements can use an upgradable storage system to manage their growing resources and supplies.
description=🏪 Traders, Visitors & Looters
description=Planned and developing systems include:
description=    • Traders
description=    • Visitors
description=    • Looters
description=    • Dynamic trading
description=    • NPC-to-NPC interactions
description=    • Settlement-to-settlement interactions
description=These systems are intended to make communities feel connected to a larger world rather than existing in isolation.
description=
description=💕 Interactive NPC Animations
description=NPCs and players can interact through contextual animations and social actions.
