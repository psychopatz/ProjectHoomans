## Sequence and Next Chunk discipline

1. Begin with `PROGRESS.md` **G3C**. Do not restart already accepted capability-generation phases or publish Workshop in order to test.
2. After G3C accepted, enter C0 with ONE bounded **production-worker conversation baseline and most consequential confirmed regression**. Do not prematurely claim C1–C6 or spawn new docs-only iterations.
3. Then C1→C2→C3→C4→C5→C6, splitting each into one source+real-worker verifiable vertical chunk. Fix safety/correctness regressions before corpus expansion.
4. A dataset is an offline reviewed *input*, never a runtime NLP interpreter. Verified context-state quality outranks corpus quantity. Existing FlowRegistry/response planning/command authority remains the source of truth.

Design: `CONVERSATIONAL_INTELLIGENCE.md`; corpus and compiler: `DATASET_CONVERSATION_PIPELINE.md`; checks: `CONVERSATION_ACCEPTANCE.md`. Earlier flow/gift/quest acceptance remains documented in `PROGRESS.md` and `HISTORY_20261018.md`.

## Non-negotiable completion

Final developer acceptance requires actual Lua-worker **multi-turn** dialogue, correct interpreted reference and context, evidenced NPC facts and durable promise, truth-preserving final reply, no unauthorized gameplay mutation, safe generator-source round-trip and measured performance. A generated node, response pool count, language dataset import or a freeform LLM response alone does not satisfy the goal.
